Alchemy matters for this character mainly because of Fletching. From Fletching 30, you need Acidic Cleansers. Weak Acidic Cleanser is made at Alchemy 25 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching), [wiki: Weak Acidic Cleanser](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Weak_Acidic_Cleanser)).

## Where to start

- Azalak in Serbule teaches Alchemy ([wiki: Azalak](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Azalak)). The research did not find the unlock cost. TODO.

## Leveling path

- Level 25. Weak Acidic Cleanser. A recipe of 2 Bottle of Water, Rotten Strawberry and Giant Spider Leg yields 2. A recipe of 3 Bottle of Water, Pyrite and Fuel Oil yields 3 ([wiki: Weak Acidic Cleanser](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Weak_Acidic_Cleanser)).
- Fletching needs Weak Acidic Cleanser from level 18 up. Skip Basic Reservoir Arrow at Fletching 18 until you have Alchemy 25 ([wiki: Weak Acidic Cleanser](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Weak_Acidic_Cleanser)).
- Potent Acidic Cleanser is needed for Reservoir Arrow at Fletching 28. The research did not look up the recipe. TODO ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).

## Dependencies

- From level 30, Fletching needs Weak, Potent or Ultimate Acidic Cleanser ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).
- Mycology gives +1 Alchemy at levels 10 and 40, and it supplies mushrooms for Alchemy ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- The Gardening research lists Alchemy for Bone Meal and rotten meat for fertilizer ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
