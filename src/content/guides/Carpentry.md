Carpentry makes Oak Dowels and the Empty Fletching Box, which Fletching cannot work without. For Archery, Carpentry supports Fletching ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).

## Where to start

- Jara in Serbule teaches the basic recipes after a quest with a bear ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Paul Vaughn teaches in Serbule Hills. Lamashu is in the Kur Mountains. Amutasa in Rahu raises the cap to 60 and 70. Norbert is on the list, but the research did not find where he is ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).

## Leveling path

- Level 3. Make Oak Dowels from 2 Oak Wood ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Level 5. Make an Empty Fletching Box from 2 Oak Wood and 1 Simple Nails ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Level 10. Make Maple Dowels from 2 Maple Wood ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Level 20. Make Cedar Dowels from 2 Cedar Wood ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Level 30. Make Spruce Dowels from 2 Spruce Wood ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- The first-craft bonus is four times the normal XP for each recipe ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Oak Wood comes from Oak Wood Logs in Serbule, Serbule Hills and north of Eltibule Keep. You chop it with a Handsaw ([wiki: Oak Wood](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Oak_Wood)). The alternative is to trade 1 Fire Dust for 4 Oak Wood with Gretchen Salas. Animal forms cannot gather wood ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- A work order from Fitz the Boatman takes 10 Oak Dowels for 450 councils. A work order from Viedesi takes 10 Empty Fletching Boxes for 854 councils at Industry 0 ([wiki: Oak Dowels](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Oak_Dowels), [wiki: Empty Fletching Box](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Empty_Fletching_Box)).

## Dependencies

- Foraging gives Oak Wood ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging)). Foraging has a synergy with Carpentry at level 20 ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging)).
- Fletching gives Carpentry +1 at levels 25 and 50 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)). Carpentry has a synergy with Fletching at levels 25 and 50 ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Toolcrafting requires Carpentry 20 ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
- Gardening gets a synergy from Carpentry at level 45 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Nice and Quality Storage Crates need a Rawhide Sheet from Tanning ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
