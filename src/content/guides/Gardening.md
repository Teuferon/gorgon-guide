Gardening has no direct link to Archery. It grows vegetables and flowers for Cooking, so it mainly supplies Cooking and brings in income ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).

## Where to start

- Therese in Serbule Keep sells seeds. Potato costs 7, Onion 9, Cabbage 12 and Beet 15 at Comfortable favor. Squash costs 19 and Broccoli 22, both at Comfortable favor. Carrot costs 27 at Friends favor. Basic Fertilizer costs 45 councils ([wiki: Therese](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Therese)).
- For levels 1 to 50, the wiki lists no trainer. Julius Patton, Therese and Kelim teach fertilizer recipes ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Kelim in Amuna teaches levels 51 to 70. Floxie in the Fae Realm teaches levels 71 to 80. Dunahain Broadarm teaches levels 81 to 90 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).

## Leveling path

- Plant a seed with a right click on a garden plot. Basic plants need a Bottle of Water, and higher plants need a Bottle of Fertilizer. You can refill water for free at the well in the center of Serbule ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Garden limits are 2 of each vegetable, 3 flowers in any mix, 5 cotton and 2 "grass" plants ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Musicians nearby randomly raise the yield. Almanacs in Serbule, Rahu, Red Wing Casino and Statehelm show the daily double-yield bonus ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Levels 0 to 19. Potato 0, Bluebell 3, Onion 5, Cabbage 10, Red Aster 10 and Violet 15 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Levels 20 to 39. Beet 20, Dahlia 20, Daisy 25, Sugarcane 25, Pansy 30, Squash 30, Marigold 35, Cotton 35 and Barley 35 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Levels 40 to 59. Broccoli 40, Poppy 40, Red Rose 45, Carrot 50, Elven Lily 50 and Green Pepper 55 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Levels 60 and up. Red Pepper 60, Corn 65 and Escarole 70 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Reaching level 100 takes 2.76 million XP in total. The first levels cost only 10 to 50 XP, so they go by fast. A first-craft bonus exists, for example 100 XP for making Basic Fertilizer from Dirt ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- An older forum advises growing two of each vegetable and three flowers at a time. This matches the wiki limits. The source is a search engine excerpt with no link.

## Dependencies

- Alchemy supplies Bone Meal and rotten meat for fertilizer ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Foraging supplies seeds ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging)). Textile Creation supplies cotton ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Cooking gets a synergy from Gardening at levels 30 and 70 ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Gardening has synergies with Carpentry at 45, with Cooking at 20, with Druid at 34 and with Plant Anatomy at 20 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Milling and Nature Appreciation are only named in the research. Their link to Gardening was not checked.
