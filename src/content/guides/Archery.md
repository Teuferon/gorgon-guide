Archery is your character's attack skill. The Animal Handling pet holds aggro while your character shoots from range. Gorgon Codex lists this pairing among the safest solo styles ([Codex: Pet Build Guide](https://www.gorgoncodex.com/guides/builds/pet-build)).

## Where to start

Archery needs no other skill ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).

- Elahil walks between the Guild House and the well in Serbule Keep. Unlocking Archery costs 100 councils at Neutral favor. He also teaches Heavy Shot, Snare Arrow and Heavy Multishot ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- Marna sells Poison Arrow 1 to 7 in the shop by the well in Serbule Keep. Poison Arrow 1 costs 450 councils at Comfortable favor. Poison Arrow 2 and 3 cost 1,450 and 2,450 councils at Friends favor ([wiki: Marna](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Marna)).
- Azalak, in the house by the portal to Eltibule, north of Serbule Keep, teaches Restorative Arrow from level 12 for 1,300 councils at Comfortable favor. He teaches Mangling Shot from level 20 for 1,700 councils at Comfortable favor ([wiki: Azalak](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Azalak)).
- Squidlips in Sun Vale teaches Acid Arrow, and Velkort in Serbule teaches Fire Arrow. Their prices are not known ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Alravesa the All-Hunter is in Amuna in Ilmari, above Selaxi. The wiki says she is there temporarily and normally lives in Rahu. Levels 51 to 60 cost 20,000 councils at Friends favor, and levels 61 to 70 cost 91,000 councils at Close Friends favor ([wiki: Alravesa](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Alravesa_the_All-Hunter)).
- Floxie in the Fae Realm teaches levels 71 to 80. Emersil the Ever-Gloating, deep below Povus, teaches levels 81 to 90. Denton Razor in Vidaria, at the Merry Bandit Camp, teaches levels 81 to 100+ and Prodigy Potential ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).

## Leveling path

Train in the zone that matches your level. The wiki lists only the area level, not the level of each monster, so check specific monsters on the in-game map ([wiki: Serbule](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Serbule), [wiki: Eltibule](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Eltibule)). An old Steam guide says the best monsters are within about 5 levels of your skill. The wiki does not confirm this ([Steam: Beginners guide to starter builds](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3668350806)). According to a community guide, these quiet spots have no competition for mobs: Ranalon Lake, Serbule Sewers and Carpal Tunnels ([projectgorgonguide.com: skills](https://projectgorgonguide.com/doku.php?id=skills)).

- Levels 1 to 10, Anagoge Island, the starting island with rats, skeletons, wolves and spiders ([wiki: Anagoge Island](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Anagoge_Island)). Level 1 gives Blitz Shot with a base damage of 13 and Heavy Shot with a base damage of 29. Blitz Shot does not cost Power. Level 4 gives Aimed Shot, a core attack with 5% accuracy. Level 5 gives Poison Arrow, Acid Arrow and Fire Arrow from three trainers ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Hangouts with Elahil give XP. Hunting deer for 4 hours and 35 favor gives 300 Archery XP and an Antler. Repairing old bows for 60 minutes and 12 favor gives 150 Archery XP. Testing arrowheads for 2 hours and 20 favor gives 150 Archery XP ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- Levels 10 to 30, Serbule and Serbule Hills, zones 1 to 30. You will find pigs, deer, wolves, tigers and Goblin Archers here. A Goblin Archer in Serbule Hills drops Advanced Arrow. Watch out for the Serbule Hills Spider Cave ([wiki: Serbule](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Serbule), [wiki: Serbule Hills](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Serbule_Hills), [wiki: Advanced Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Advanced_Arrow)). Level 7 gives Hook Shot with a taunt. Level 8 gives Multishot and Heavy Multishot, which are area attacks. Multishot always uses 5 arrows. Level 11 gives Snare Arrow, which mesmerizes for 10 seconds. Level 12 gives Restorative Arrow, which heals 55 and needs Azalak. Level 13 gives Long Shot with a range of 45 m. Level 20 gives Mangling Shot, which stuns ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Levels 20 to 45, Eltibule, zones 20 to 50. You will find Gnashers, Tor-Urak, Goblin Archers, the Goblin Dungeon and Hogan's Basement here ([wiki: Eltibule](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Eltibule)).
- Levels 30 to 50, Sun Vale and the Kur Mountains, zones 30 to 45 and 30 to 50. The Kur Mountains are cold, so you need a heat source ([Sun Vale](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Sun_Vale), [Kur Mountains](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Kur_Mountains)). Level 30 gives Heavy Shot 3 with 183 damage. Level 45 gives Poison Arrow 5, Acid Arrow 5 and Fire Arrow 5 ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Levels 50 to 60, the Ilmari Desert with Amuna and Rahu. You need water because of "Dying of Thirst". Ilmari was reworked in February 2026, so the monster list on the wiki may be out of date ([Ilmari](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Ilmari), [Rahu](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Rahu)). Level 50 gives Heavy Shot 4 with 418 damage, Aimed Shot 4 with 170 damage and Mangling Shot 4. Level 51 gives Blitz Shot 6 and Snare Arrow 5 ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Levels 70 to 80, Gazluk and the Fae Realm. Gazluk has one safe zone, New Prestonbule Cave ([Gazluk](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gazluk), [Fae Realm](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fae_Realm)). Level 75 gives Heavy Shot 6, Poison Arrow 8 and Fire Arrow 8 ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Levels 90 and up, Vidaria, zone 90 to 95, with Denton Razor ([wiki: Vidaria](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Vidaria)).
- Each ability needs a minimum arrow tier. Blitz Shot 1 and 2 and Aimed Shot 1 consume "Beginner's Arrow or better". Aimed Shot 2 at level 18 and Blitz Shot 3 at level 21 need Basic Arrow. Blitz Shot 4 at level 30, Aimed Shot 3 at level 35 and Multishot 3 at level 40 need Advanced Arrow. Blitz Shot 5 at level 42 and Aimed Shot 4 at level 50 need Expert's Arrow. Blitz Shot 6 at level 51 needs Masterwork Arrow ([Basic Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Basic_Arrow), [Advanced Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Advanced_Arrow), [Expert's Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Expert%27s_Arrow), [Masterwork Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Masterwork_Arrow)).
- Elahil sells Beginner's Arrow for 3, Basic Arrow for 7 and Advanced Arrow for 15 councils at Comfortable favor ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)). NPCs do not sell Expert's Arrow or Masterwork Arrow, so you have to craft them at Fletching or buy them from players ([wiki: Expert's Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Expert%27s_Arrow), [wiki: Masterwork Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Masterwork_Arrow)).

## Dependencies

- Fletching makes arrows and gives +1 Archery at levels 15, 29, 43 and 75 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)). Fletching itself needs Carpentry, Alchemy, Foraging, Butchering and Skinning.
- Bowyery gives +1 Archery at level 75. Its trainer, Denton Razor, is in Vidaria ([wiki: Bowyery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Bowyery)).
- Anatomy raises your critical hits. Every Archery attack can crit, based on your Anatomy knowledge against that target type ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Archery gets synergies from Fletching at 15, 29, 43 and 75, from Bowyery at 75, from Crossbow at 25, 50 and 75, from Human Anatomy at 19, and from Meditation at 25 ([wiki: Archery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Archery)).
- Animal Handling is the pair for the pet build. Mentalism and Druid are alternative second skills. See their files.
