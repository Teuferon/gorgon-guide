Fletching makes arrows, which Archery uses up. From Archery 42, you need Expert's Arrow. NPCs do not sell it, so you have to craft it or buy it from players ([wiki: Expert's Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Expert%27s_Arrow)).

## Where to start

- Elahil in Serbule Keep teaches Fletching in the range 1 to 50. Unlocking costs 500 councils at Comfortable favor ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- The quest "Feathers For Elahil" at Neutral favor asks for 3 Feathers and gives 500 Beginner's Arrows as a one-time reward ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil), [wiki: Beginner's Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Beginners_Arrow)).
- Therese sells a Handsaw for gathering wood, for 75 councils ([wiki: Therese](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Therese)).
- Bellema Deftwhisper is in New Prestonbule Cave in Gazluk, next to the fire to the right of the entrance. She teaches levels 51 to 60. Unlocking costs 20,000 councils at Friends favor. Individual Amazing recipes cost 4,800 to 7,733 councils ([wiki: Bellema Deftwhisper](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Bellema_Deftwhisper)). The trip to Gazluk goes through the Kur Mountains and is much longer than the rest of the route ([wiki: Gazluk](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gazluk)).
- Floxie in the Fae Realm teaches levels 61 to 80. Mara Woodwright in Statehelm teaches levels 81 to 91 and recipes up to 118 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).

## Leveling path

Total XP to level 50 is 78,010 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)). Applying the 16 times level rule to the first-craft bonuses gives an estimated 20,700 XP, about a quarter of that total. This is an estimate from the recipes on the wiki.

- Levels 1 to 9. Beginner's Arrowheads at level 1 need Flinty Rock, which costs 20 councils from Elahil ([wiki: Flinty Rock](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Flinty_Rock)). Beginner's Arrow Shafts at level 2 need Oak Dowels and an Empty Fletching Box. Elahil sells both for 100 councils each at Comfortable favor. You can also make them with Carpentry, where the dowel is at level 3 and the box is at level 5 ([wiki: Empty Fletching Box](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Empty_Fletching_Box), [wiki: Oak Dowels](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Oak_Dowels)).
- Beginner's Arrow at level 3, Barbed at 4, Long at 6, Dense at 7 with Antler, Reservoir at 8 with Beakers, and Snare at 9 with Spiderweb. All of them need Feather. Antler comes from Elahil's hangout "Bow-hunting for stags" ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- Feathers drop from chickens, wild turkeys, spiders, wolves and Deinonychus. You can also skin Feathers from Chicken, Wild Chicken and Wild Turkey with Skinning ([wiki: Feather](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Feather)).
- Using the same 16 times level rule, the first-craft bonuses for tiers 1 to 9 add up to 794 XP. This is an estimate, roughly 63% of the XP to level 10 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).
- Levels 10 to 19. Basic Arrow Shafts at level 10 need Oak Dowels, an Empty Fletching Box and Copper Ore. The organic variant at level 11 uses Spider Leg instead of ore. Marna gives 8 Spider Legs for the hangout "Check spider traps", which takes 60 minutes and costs 12 favor ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching), [wiki: Marna](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Marna)).
- Basic Arrowheads at level 10 come from Flinty Rock and Basic Fish Scales. The research did not find a source for Fish Scales. Prepared Fletching at level 12 comes from Feathers and Basic Wood Glue. Wood Glue costs 15 councils from Elahil and Sie Antry. Elahil sells finished Prepared Fletchings for 100 councils ([wiki: Basic Wood Glue](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Basic_Wood_Glue), [wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- Basic Arrow at level 13, Barbed at 14, Long at 16, Dense at 17 with Antler, and Snare at 19 with Spiderweb and Basic Wood Glue. Fletching 15 gives +1 Archery. Basic Reservoir Arrow at level 18 needs Weak Acidic Cleanser from Alchemy 25, so skip it for now ([wiki: Weak Acidic Cleanser](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Weak_Acidic_Cleanser)).
- Levels 20 to 29. Advanced Arrowheads at level 20 need Flinty Rock and 2 Femur. You get Femur from skeletons or from Butchering ([wiki: Butchering](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Butchering)). Advanced Arrow Shafts Mineral at level 20 need Maple Dowels, a box and 3 Gold Nugget. The organic variant at level 21 uses Giant Spider Leg instead. Carpentry makes Maple Dowels at level 10, and Elahil sells them for 200 councils at Friends favor ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry), [wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- Advanced Arrow at level 22, Barbed at 24, Long at 26, Dense at 27 with Copper Ore, Reservoir at 28 with Potent Acidic Cleanser, and Snare at 29 with 2 Spiderweb. Fletching 29 gives +1 Archery. Advanced Arrow is the first tier that Blitz Shot 4 and Aimed Shot 3 require ([wiki: Advanced Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Advanced_Arrow)).
- Levels 30 to 49, Expert's and Masterwork tiers. Almost every recipe from level 30 needs Weak, Potent or Ultimate Acidic Cleanser, so without Alchemy you can make almost nothing ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)). Weak Acidic Cleanser is made at Alchemy 25 from 2 Bottle of Water, Rotten Strawberry and Giant Spider Leg, which yields 2, or from 3 Bottle of Water, Pyrite and Fuel Oil, which yields 3 ([wiki: Weak Acidic Cleanser](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Weak_Acidic_Cleanser)).
- Expert's Arrowheads at level 30 come from Flinty Rock, Small Dinosaur Scale and Weak Acidic Cleanser. Elegant Fletching at level 32 comes from Feathers, Advanced Wood Glue and Weak Acidic Cleanser. Advanced Wood Glue costs 37 councils from Elahil at Friends favor.
- Expert's Arrow Shafts Mineral at level 30 need Maple Dowels, Silver Ore and Weak Acidic Cleanser. The organic variant at level 31 needs Giant Spider Leg and Weak Acidic Cleanser. Expert's Arrow unlocks at level 33. Fletching 43 gives +1 Archery.
- Masterwork Arrowheads at level 40 need Fulgurite. Masterwork Arrow Shafts at levels 40 and 41 need either Cedar Dowels and Silver Ore, or Cat Eyeball and Potent Acidic Cleanser. Carpentry makes Cedar Dowels at level 20, and Elahil sells them for 400 councils at Close Friends favor. Cat Eyeball drops from Old Fangsworth, among others ([wiki: Old Fangsworth](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Old_Fangsworth)).
- Elahil sells Expert's Arrowheads for 800 councils at Friends favor and Masterwork Arrowheads for 1,000 councils at Close Friends favor, so you can buy the heads instead of crafting them ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
- Levels 50 to 60. Bellema in Gazluk teaches for 20,000 councils. Her Amazing Arrowheads cost 4,800 councils, while Elahil sells them for 1,200 at Best Friends favor, so buying from Elahil is cheaper ([wiki: Bellema Deftwhisper](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Bellema_Deftwhisper), [wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)). Precision Fletching at level 50 needs Expert Wood Glue, which costs 75 councils from Sie Antry ([wiki: Sie Antry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Sie_Antry)).
- A Drying Box is made from an Empty Fletching Box, a dowel and an extra component. Arrow shafts are dried in it ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).
- The Drying Box works only in sunny weather, from 6:00 to 17:59. The number of boxes that can dry at the same time is 1 at level 1, 2 at level 10, 3 at level 20, 4 at level 30, 5 at level 40, 6 at level 50 and 7 at level 60 ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).
- Drying time is 1 minute for Beginner, 5 for Basic, 7 for Advanced, 15 for Expert, 20 for Masterwork, 24 for Amazing and 30 for Astonishing. Do not build boxes on a slope. A box that sinks in can block new ones from being placed. The `/zapresources fletching` command helps, and it gives a confirmation code ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).

## Cheapest route

1. Until Archery is around 40, do not craft arrows. Buy Beginner's, Basic and Advanced arrows from Elahil. The highest tier you buy is Advanced ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)).
2. Keep Fletching up to level 50, the range Elahil teaches. Level mainly with first-craft bonuses. One craft of each recipe is enough.
3. The goal is Fletching 33 before Archery 42, because Blitz Shot 5 needs Expert's Arrow, which NPCs do not sell ([wiki: Expert's Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Expert%27s_Arrow)).
4. Gather materials from drops and hangouts instead of buying them. These are Feathers, Spider Leg, Femur and Antler.
5. Buy arrowheads from Elahil and Sie Antry when they are cheaper than the raw material.
6. Sell extra arrows to Elahil, who buys more than just Beginner's Arrows, or turn them in as Work Orders ([wiki: Elahil](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Elahil)). Fitz the Boatman takes 10,000 Basic Arrows for 1,484 councils at Industry 0. Thimble Pete takes 10,000 Advanced Arrows for 1,970 councils at Industry 10, and 10,000 Expert's Arrows for 3,342 councils at Industry 20 ([wiki: Basic Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Basic_Arrow), [wiki: Advanced Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Advanced_Arrow), [wiki: Expert's Arrow](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Expert%27s_Arrow)).

## Dependencies

- Carpentry supplies dowels and the Empty Fletching Box, which Fletching cannot work without. Fletching gives Carpentry +1 at levels 25 and 50 ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry), [wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).
- Alchemy supplies Acidic Cleansers from Fletching 30 ([wiki: Weak Acidic Cleanser](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Weak_Acidic_Cleanser)).
- Foraging supplies wood for Carpentry ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging)).
- Mining gives Copper Ore, Silver Ore and Gold Nugget. The research did not track these sources ([wiki: Fletching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Fletching)).
- Butchering and Skinning supply Femur, Feathers and Cat Eyeball ([wiki: Butchering](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Butchering), [wiki: Skinning](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Skinning)).
- Bowyery gives +1 Archery at level 75, but its trainer is in Vidaria and it is not a priority. Tuneup Kits for bows add accuracy and critical damage from level 15 ([wiki: Bowyery](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Bowyery)).
