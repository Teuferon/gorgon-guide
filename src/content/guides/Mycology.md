Mycology has no direct use for Archery. It matters mainly for Druid, which gets a synergy and a +1 Druid bonus at level 45 from Mycology ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology), [wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)). It also supplies mushrooms for Cooking and Alchemy.

## Where to start

- Low levels are taught by Mushroom Jack and Charles Thompson in Serbule, and by Voo and Jaw in Myconian Cave ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Hemmit Magmagrip in New Prestonbule Cave teaches levels 51 to 70. Backfat in the Fae Realm teaches levels 71 to 80. Dunahain Broadarm or Strom Farblood teach levels 81 to 90 ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Echur in Serbule offers the hangout "Explore new mind-altering compounds", which gives 25 Mycology XP and 4 Blusher Mushroom ([wiki: Echur](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Echur)).

## Leveling path

- Early mushrooms Parasol, Mycena and Boletus are found in Anagoge, Serbule and Myconian Cave ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Mid-tier mushrooms Field, Blusher, Milk Cap and Blood are found in Eltibule, Ranalon Den and the Kur Mountains ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Gathering mushrooms gives XP that rises with the tier. Parasol gives 15 XP, and Field Mushroom gives 96 XP. The XP table runs from 10 XP at level 1 to 30,000 XP at a level near 100 ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- The first-craft bonus is, for example, 35 XP instead of 5 for Parasol Mushroom Flakes ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Mycology makes Spore Bombs, poisons and potions ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).

## Dependencies

- Mycology gives +1 Alchemy at levels 10 and 40, and it supplies mushrooms for Alchemy ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Mycology gives +1 Druid at level 45 ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology), [wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Foraging gives Mycology bonus levels at 30 and 55 ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging)).
- Gardening gives Mycology a synergy at level 60 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
