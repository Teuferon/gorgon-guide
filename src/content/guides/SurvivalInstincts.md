According to the wiki, Survival Instincts is a skill only for animal characters, and it requires Beast Forms. If your character is not an animal, you cannot use the skill, and Armor Patching is the replacement ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts), [wiki: Armor Patching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Armor_Patching)).

## Where to start

- Mittens in Fox Outpost in the Kur Mountains is one of the trainers. The research does not say what Mittens teaches or what it costs ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).
- Sanja in the Kur Mountains teaches levels 51 to 70. Backfat teaches levels 71 to 80. Serendipity Cheddar and Hamilton in Statehelm Sewers teach levels 81 to 100 ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).

## Leveling path

- The abilities are Toughen Up, which restores armor, Toxic Flesh and Lend Grace ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).
- XP is a table from 5 XP at level 1 to 379,301 XP at level 50. The wiki does not describe another leveling method ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).

## Dependencies

- Survival Instincts requires Beast Forms ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).
- Survival Instincts gets a synergy from Animal Handling at level 42, and it has synergies with Druid at levels 17 and 55 ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).
- Armor Patching is the replacement for a character that is not an animal ([wiki: Armor Patching](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Armor_Patching)).

## Open questions

- The open question for your character is which race and form it has. That decides whether Survival Instincts can be used. Animal forms also cannot gather wood ([wiki: Carpentry](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Carpentry)).
