Druid is a permanent character change, and it needs a combat skill at level 50. For Archery, Druid gives healing, Toxinball, and Deer Form or Raven Form ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).

## Where to start

- The requirement is any combat skill at level 50. The altar in Druid Camp, at the north tip of Sun Vale, accepts your vow only after that. The wiki stresses this with "PERMANENT CHARACTER ALTERATION" ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Silvia in Druid Camp in Sun Vale teaches the basic abilities and Toxinball 1 to 6. Toxinball 1 costs 450 councils at Neutral favor, and Toxinball 2 costs 1,450 councils at Comfortable favor ([wiki: Silvia](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Silvia)).
- Azalak in Serbule teaches Cloud Sight and Pulse of Life ([wiki: Azalak](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Azalak)).
- Lady Windsong in Rahu unlocks levels 51 to 70. Raina in the Fae Realm teaches levels 71 to 80. Reinath teaches levels 81 to 90. Parella in Statehelm teaches levels 81 to 100 ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Humanoid druids need a wooden weapon: a staff, a lute or a club ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).

## Leveling path

- Toxinball is a core Poison attack that starts at level 5 ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Regrowth, a major heal, unlocks at level 10. Pulse of Life, a minor heal, unlocks at level 15 ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Deer Form at level 25 and Raven Form at level 50 are for travel, healing, attacks and the Heart Thorn debuff ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Some Druid events do not give Druid XP ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- The choice cannot be undone. Make it only after you have tried Toxinball and Regrowth in play.
- Druid skills cannot be used in wolf form, so the combination with Lycanthropy is not possible yet ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).

## Dependencies

- A combat skill at level 50. Archery meets this if it is at 50 ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)).
- Druid gets synergies from Mycology at level 45, from Survival Instincts at 45, from Deer at 21 and 61, and from Staff at 47 ([wiki: Druid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Druid)). Mycology gives +1 Druid at level 45 ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Survival Instincts has synergies with Druid at levels 17 and 55 ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).
- The Foraging, Gardening and First Aid pages list synergies with Druid at levels 22, 34 and 29 ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging), [wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening), [wiki: First Aid](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/First_Aid)).
