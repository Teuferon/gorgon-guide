Animal Handling adds a pet to Archery. The pet holds aggro while your character shoots from range. Gorgon Codex calls this pair the most popular pet build and recommends it for level 100 ([Codex: Pet Build Guide](https://www.gorgoncodex.com/guides/builds/pet-build)).

## Where to start

- Gisli is in Serbule Keep, by the north gate next to the stables. The skill unlocks for 2,000 councils with no favor requirement ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling), [wiki: Gisli](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gisli)).
- Gisli also teaches levels 51 to 60 for 20,000 councils at Close Friends favor. He teaches levels 61 to 70 for 100,000 councils at Best Friends favor ([wiki: Gisli](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gisli)).
- Crelpin in the Fae Realm teaches levels 71 to 80 and Tame Bee. Emersil under Povus and Sir Abbley in Statehelm teach levels 81 to 100 ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).
- Stable slots 1 to 3 are free. Slot 4 costs 100 councils, and slot 14 costs 10,000,000 councils. Mounts share slots with pets, so a mount in slot 1 can block your first pet ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).

## Leveling path

- The hangout "Help Gisli train a new animal" takes 2 hours and 20 favor, and it gives 250 Animal Handling XP ([wiki: Gisli](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gisli)).
- You gain XP by using pet abilities in combat ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)). Each pet levels on its own. Once a pet has enough XP, you have to visit a trainer and pay a fee ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).
- Levels 1 to 9. Tame Rat at level 1 needs cheese. Cheese grows on the starting island, Anagoge ([wiki: Anagoge Island](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Anagoge_Island), [wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)). Level 3 gives Sic 'Em, level 4 gives Feed Pet, level 5 gives That'll Do, and level 6 gives Get It Off Me. Get It Off Me is a taunt and a defensive mode. Level 7 gives Monstrous Rage.
- Level 8 gives Shrill Command, a core attack with a range of 30 m that does not need a pet. Level 10 gives Clever Trick. Level 15 gives Unnatural Wrath, which needs an Anchor Rune and damages your pet. Level 30 gives Mark Weakness, a signature debuff ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).
- Tame Big Cat (level 20) is learned at the Cat Shrine in northeast Eltibule by sacrificing a String or a Cat Eyeball. Tame Bear (level 40) is learned at the Bear Shrine in the Kur Mountains by sacrificing a Perch. Tame Bee needs a Bee-Lover's Bouquet at level 70 ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).
- Old Fangsworth is a named tiger in Serbule. Its tame level is 20. It has 635 health, is weak to Fire, Poison and Trauma, and respawns after 3 minutes. It drops, among other things, Beginner's Arrow, Cat Eyeball and Crude Animal Skin ([wiki: Old Fangsworth](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Old_Fangsworth)).
- Zones are the same as for Archery, because the pet fights alongside you ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).
- Pet care takes work outside combat. You have to tame the pet, keep its Happiness up, and pay for its level ([Codex: Pet Build Guide](https://www.gorgoncodex.com/guides/builds/pet-build)). A pet can die in hard fights, and its death halves its Happiness ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).

## Dependencies

- Archery is the paired skill. Ability range is measured between you and the pet ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)). Archery needs arrows, which take up inventory space ([Codex: Pet Build Guide](https://www.gorgoncodex.com/guides/builds/pet-build)).
- Skinning gives +1 Animal Handling at level 15 ([wiki: Skinning](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Skinning)).
- Survival Instincts gets a synergy from Animal Handling at level 42 ([wiki: Survival Instincts](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Survival_Instincts)).

## Open questions

- The sources disagree on how to unlock the skill. The wiki says Gisli, for 2,000 councils. Codex says the skill is first learned from Crelpin in the Fae Realm, and that Gisli is the XP trainer at levels 50 and 60 ([wiki: Gisli](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gisli), [Codex: Animal Handling](https://www.gorgoncodex.com/guides/skills/animalhandling)). The wiki gives both the price and the favor, so it is more reliable. Check in the game. You probably already have the skill by around level 15, so this question is probably settled.
- According to the FAQ, Animal Handling is "no longer recommended as an optional secondary" after the March 2023 changes. The same FAQ says that in 2025 the Archery pairing was adjusted to be good, and it says "Go Archery/Animal Handling if you want a pet!" ([projectgorgonguide.com: faq](https://projectgorgonguide.com/doku.php?id=faq)). The sources agree that the pairing makes the most sense if you want a pet.
- The wiki text for Bees gives level 70. The table lists Freeze Wasp from level 35 and tameable bees up to level 100. Not verified ([wiki: Animal Handling](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Animal_Handling)).
