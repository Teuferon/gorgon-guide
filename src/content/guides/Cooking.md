Cooking makes food and drinks with regeneration effects. Drinks generally give Power regeneration, which Archery uses up ([wiki: Gourmand](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gourmand)).

## Where to start

- Fainor in Serbule Keep sells most of the recipes that need a stove or a campfire ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Durstin Tallow in the Tapestry Inn in Serbule Hills teaches levels 3 to 24 ([wiki: Serbule Hills](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Serbule_Hills)).
- Therese teaches Apple Juice at level 10. Julius Patton teaches Grape Juice at level 13 ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Laura Neth teaches levels 30 to 45, Selaxi teaches levels 51 to 60, and Floxie teaches levels 61 to 80. Dunahain Broadarm or Deville Neth teach levels 81 to 90 ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).

## Leveling path

- The first-craft bonus is about four times the normal XP. For example, it is 40 XP instead of 10 XP for level 0 recipes. Eating a new food for the first time gives Gourmand XP, usually 10 times the Meal Level ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking), [wiki: Gourmand](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gourmand)).
- Level 0. Baked Potato, made from Potato and Salt, needs a stove or a campfire. Also Potato Wedges, Boiled Crab, Fried Clownfish, Honey Ham, Watery Mushroom Soup and Plain Porridge ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Levels 1 to 3. Hard-Boiled Egg, Candied Apple and Fish Cakes ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Level 5. Each recipe gives 20 XP. Bacon, Flatbread, Hash Browns, Onion Rings, Strawberry Bowl and Bland Baked Chicken ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Level 10. Apple Juice from 3 Red Apple, 2 Sugar and Water. Also Boiled Cabbage, Fried Potato Sticks and Sun Vale Tea ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- More recipes from a fan article, without prices. Clownfish Chowder from level 7, Venison Jerky from 10, Cabbage Soup from 15, Cat Stew Cup from 16, Fish and Chips from 20, Black Soup and Loaded Baked Potato from 25, Basic Shark-Fin Soup and Eel Stir-Fry from 30, Mantis-Stuffed Mushroom, Chicken-Rabbit Rice and Pickled Pike from 35, and Almond-Crusted Walleye and Yeti Stick from 40 ([Magic Game World](https://www.magicgameworld.com/?p=143958)).
- Baking has its own mechanic, with timing windows for dough. Timing affects quality. The secret window is random for each account ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).

## Cheapest route

- The wiki lists cheap recipes at levels 0 to 10 ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- The wiki advises buying several recipes and making one of each, so every recipe gets the first-craft bonus ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
- Salt, Sugar and Flour are the basics. You mostly buy them from Fainor ([Magic Game World](https://www.magicgameworld.com/?p=143958)).
- Gardening gives potatoes and onions. Therese sells seeds from 7 councils ([wiki: Therese](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Therese)).
- Kill pigs and deer near Serbule, cook them, and sell them to Fainor or to players. The Making Money page is from 2020 ([wiki: Making Money](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Making_Money)).

## Dependencies

- Gardening supplies vegetables. It also gives Cooking a synergy at levels 30 and 70 ([wiki: Gardening](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Gardening)).
- Butchering supplies meat and gives +1 Cooking at levels 20 and 40. Its synergies with Cooking are also at 20 and 40 ([wiki: Butchering](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Butchering)).
- Foraging supplies fruit and has a synergy with Cooking at level 40 ([wiki: Foraging](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Foraging)).
- Skinning gives +1 Cooking at level 60 ([wiki: Skinning](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Skinning)).
- Mycology supplies mushrooms for Cooking ([wiki: Mycology](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Mycology)).
- Cheesemaking and Fishing are other sources of ingredients. The research did not track them ([wiki: Cooking](https://wiki.projectgorgon.com/wiki/Cooking)).
